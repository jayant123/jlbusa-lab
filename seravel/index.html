    The workshop

    Three jewelers and one long bench

    SERAVEL is a small workshop. Three jewelers make every piece by hand. Nothing is cast in bulk.

    We set each stone under a microscope. The claws are shaped to the stone you chose. Then we polish the ring by hand in three stages.

    All our gold is recycled 18k. Our diamonds are lab-grown. Our sapphires and emeralds come from suppliers who name the mine. We keep that paperwork for every stone and send you a copy.

    From the journal

    Short notes on choosing and caring for fine jewelry.

    • How to clean a ring at home

      Warm water, a drop of dish soap and a soft toothbrush. You need nothing else.

      Read more

      Soak the ring for ten minutes. Brush under the stone where lotion and soap build up. Rinse it and dry it with a soft cloth. Skip toothpaste and baking soda. Both scratch gold.

    • Diamond, sapphire or emerald

      Each stone wears differently. Here is what to expect from each one.

      Read more

      Diamond is the hardest and suits daily wear. Sapphire is nearly as hard and keeps its color for life. Emerald is softer. It suits a ring you take off for the gym and for housework.

    • Why we use four claws

      Four claws show more of the stone. Six claws hold it more tightly.

      Read more

      A round stone in four claws looks a little square from above. It also lets in more light from the sides. We check the claws when you send a ring in for cleaning.

    Questions

    How long does a ring take?

    About four weeks from your order to your door. Engraved rings take the same time.

    Can I change the size later?

    Yes. Resizing is free in the first 60 days. After that we charge for the work.

    Are your diamonds natural or lab-grown?

    Our diamonds are lab-grown. They are chemically the same as mined diamonds.

    Do you ship outside the US?

    Not yet. Every order ships insured within the US.

    Can I return a piece?

    You can return an unworn piece within 30 days. Engraved rings cannot be returned.

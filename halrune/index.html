    Ch 03

    How we make a driver

    1. The diaphragm We press each one from a thin sheet in a heated mold. It is light and stiff. That is what keeps the sound clean.
    2. The motor A neodymium magnet sits behind the coil. We glue it by hand and let it cure overnight.
    3. Matching We measure every driver. Pairs go into the same headphones only when they match within half a decibel.

    Ch 04

    The wood

    We use walnut, ash and oak from two mills in Pennsylvania. We visit both each year. The cups are turned from solid blocks. The speaker cabinets are veneered by hand.

    Ch 06

    Setting up the Monitors

    Ten minutes and a tape measure.

    1. 1

      Make a triangle

      Put the speakers as far apart as you sit from them. Turn them in a little toward you.

    2. 2

      Lift them up

      The tweeters should be at ear height. Stands or books both work.

    3. 3

      Leave a gap

      Keep the backs 20 cm from the wall. Closer gives more bass. Further gives a cleaner sound.

    Ch 08

    From the journal

    Notes on listening and on looking after your gear.

    • Open-back or closed-back?

      Open-back headphones let sound out. That is why they feel so spacious.

      Read more

      Closed cups trap sound and block the room. That helps on a train. At home the open design sounds more like speakers in front of you. People nearby will hear a little of your music.

    • Caring for wood cups

      Wipe them with a dry cloth. Once a year use a drop of furniture oil.

      Read more

      Keep the cups away from radiators and direct sun. Wood moves with the seasons. A small change in color over the years is normal. It is part of the charm.

    • Where to put your speakers

      Most rooms sound better with the speakers off the back wall.

      Read more

      Corners make bass boom. Bare walls make echoes. A rug and a bookshelf help a lot. Move the speakers a few centimeters at a time and listen again.

    Questions

    Can I try the products at home?

    Yes. You have 30 days to return anything in its box. We pay for the return label.

    Do the Monitors need an amplifier?

    No. The amplifiers are built in. Plug in power and a source and you are ready.

    Will the One work with my phone?

    Yes. The cable has a 3.5 mm plug. Use an adapter if your phone has no headphone jack.

    Can I replace the ear pads?

    Yes. They pull off by hand. New pads clip on in a few seconds.

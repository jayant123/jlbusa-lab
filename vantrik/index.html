    What the foam does

    Each step squashes the midsole a little. The foam springs back before your next step. Softer foam feels kind on day one. It also wears out sooner.

    Our foam sits in the middle. It feels firm when new. After a week it shapes to your stride. It keeps that feel for about 500 miles.

    Journal

    Notes on shoes and the people who wear them out.

    • How to clean knit uppers

      Cold water and a soft brush do most of the work. Skip the washing machine.

      Read more

      Take the laces and insoles out first. Brush the knit with cold soapy water. Rinse with a damp cloth. Stuff the shoes with paper and let them dry away from heat. Heat can warp the foam.

    • When to replace your shoes

      Look at the foam. Not the upper.

      Read more

      Most foam lasts 400 to 600 miles. Press your thumb into the midsole by the heel. Fine creases are normal. Deep wrinkles mean the foam has gone flat. Your knees will notice before your eyes do.

    • Why we make small runs

      We make each colorway in batches of 600 pairs. Then we stop.

      Read more

      Small runs mean less dead stock. They also let us change things between batches. The VK-1 heel tab got stiffer after the first run. A reader asked for it.

    The movement

    A movement you can service for decades

    The Meridian 40 runs on a self-winding movement with 25 jewels. It beats four times a second. A full wind keeps it running for about three days off the wrist.

    The bridges carry Côtes de Genève stripes. The main plate is finished with small overlapping circles. Nobody sees these from the front. We do them anyway.

    Our story

    Why we make only one watch

    OSTREL started with a simple idea. Make one watch and keep making it better. No new model every spring. No limited editions.

    The Meridian 40 is the watch we wanted to wear every day. It is thin enough for a shirt cuff. It is tough enough for a weekend outside. The dial is easy to read at a glance.

    Each watch is assembled and regulated by hand in small batches. It is tested for a week before it ships.

    Service and warranty

    Plain terms. No fine print.

    • Five years of cover

      We repair the watch at no cost when a part fails from a fault in how we made it. This covers the movement, the case and the crystal. Straps and normal wear are not included.

    • A full service every five to seven years

      We take the movement apart, clean it, oil it and regulate it. A service costs a flat fee and takes about three weeks. Shipping both ways is on us.

    • Straps you can change yourself

      Every strap has quick-release pins. Swap a calf strap for the bracelet in under a minute. No tools needed.

    From the journal

    Short notes on owning a mechanical watch.

    • How to wind an automatic watch

      Your wrist does most of the work. A few turns of the crown help on the first day.

      Read more

      Give the crown about thirty turns when the watch has stopped. That starts it with a good reserve. After that, normal wear keeps it wound. Leave it on a shelf for more than three days and it will stop again. Wind it and set the time.

    • Why a watch gains or loses time

      A mechanical watch is never perfect. A few seconds a day is normal.

      Read more

      Temperature, position and how much the spring is wound all change the rate. Leave the watch crown up overnight and it often slows a little. Crown down speeds it up. You can use this to nudge it back on time.

    • Leather or steel?

      Leather feels warm and light. Steel shrugs off water and sweat.

      Read more

      Calf leather softens over the first month. It is the more comfortable choice for an office. The steel bracelet suits summer and travel. Many owners buy both and swap with the quick-release pins.

    Questions

    How accurate is the Meridian 40?

    We regulate each watch to within about five seconds a day. It is tested in five positions before it ships.

    Can I swim with it?

    Yes. It is water resistant to 100 m. Keep the crown pushed in. Rinse it in fresh water after the sea.

    How long does an order take?

    Stock builds ship in 3 to 5 business days. Engraved watches take about a week longer.

    Can I return an engraved watch?

    Engraved watches can't be returned. Every other order can come back within thirty days.

    Will it fit a small wrist?

    Use the wrist check in the configurator. The lugs are 47 mm from tip to tip. That suits most wrists from about 15 cm.

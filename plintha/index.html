            How it is made

            Every module stands on a plinth

            The plinth is solid oak. It is smoked to a deep brown and finished with hard wax oil. It sits 4 cm in from the edge. So the sofa looks like it floats a little above the floor.

            The frame is kiln-dried beech with corner blocks. The seat is pocket springs under high-density foam. The back cushions are a feather and fiber mix in channels. They keep their shape.

            From the journal

            Notes on choosing and living with a sofa.

            • How to measure your room for a sofa

              Measure the wall first. Then measure every door and turn on the way in.

              Read more

              Leave at least 60 cm free at each end of the sofa. Leave 45 cm between the sofa and a coffee table. Our modules fit through any door wider than 75 cm. They arrive one at a time.

            • Boucle, wool, linen or velvet

              Each fabric wears in its own way. Pick the one that fits how you live.

              Read more

              Boucle hides marks well and suits pets. Wool is warm and hard wearing. Linen softens with every wash and creases a little. Velvet is the softest. It shows the direction of the pile.

            • Caring for an oak plinth

              Dust the plinth with a dry cloth. Once a year it likes a little oil.

              Read more

              Use a hard wax oil made for furniture. Rub a thin coat in with a soft cloth. Wipe off what does not soak in after ten minutes. Let it dry overnight before anyone sits down.

            Questions

            Can I add modules later?

            Yes. Every module clips to every other module. Fabric batches can vary a little. So order extra pieces in the same fabric within a year if you can.

            How do the modules stay together?

            Steel clips under the seat cushions hold each module to the next. They release with one hand.

            Can I wash the covers?

            Yes. Every cover zips off. Wool, linen and boucle can go in a cold gentle wash. Velvet should be dry cleaned.

            Will it fit through my door?

            Each module arrives on its own. The largest is the chaise at 90 by 165 cm. It fits through a door 75 cm wide.

            What if I change my mind?

            You have 30 days from delivery. We collect the sofa and refund the price minus the delivery cost.

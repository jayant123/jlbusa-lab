    From the journal

    Short notes on wearing perfume.

    • How to make a scent last longer

      Put it on your skin and not on your clothes. Spray it after a shower while your skin is still warm.

      Read more

      Dry skin holds scent poorly. An unscented lotion first helps a lot. Spray the wrists and the back of the neck. Do not rub your wrists together. Rubbing breaks down the top notes faster.

    • Top, heart and base notes

      A perfume changes as it dries. Here is what happens in the first six hours.

      Read more

      Top notes are what you smell first. They fade in about fifteen minutes. Heart notes come next and last a few hours. Base notes stay longest. They are what people remember after you leave the room.

    • Why our bottles are colored glass

      Colored glass protects perfume from light. It also looks good on a shelf.

      Read more

      Sunlight breaks perfume down over time. Tinted glass filters some of that light. Keep your bottle out of direct sun all the same. A drawer or a shelf away from the window is best.
